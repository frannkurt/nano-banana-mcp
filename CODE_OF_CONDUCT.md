# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Criticise the code, not the person.

## What's expected

- Give people the benefit of the doubt, especially across a language barrier. This project's maintainer works in
  Spanish and most contributors won't.
- Say what you observed and what you changed. Vague complaints aren't feedback.
- Accept that "no" is a legitimate answer, particularly for anything touching credentials or the cost gate.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information — including pasting someone's session tokens, account email or project
  identifiers into an issue.
- Sustained disruption: reopening settled decisions, or demanding unpaid work from a maintainer.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and commit messages — anywhere the project is being worked on,
and anywhere someone is representing it publicly.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainer, [@frannkurt](https://github.com/frannkurt), via a
[private advisory](https://github.com/frannkurt/nano-banana-mcp/security/advisories/new) or GitHub direct message.
Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may edit, delete, or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests that don't fit this document,
and may block accounts for serious or repeated behaviour. Being right about the code doesn't exempt anyone.
